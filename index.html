Welcome, Aviators!

Welcome to the official home of the Piedmont University Drone Club! We are a community of students passionate about drone technology, aerial photography, and the future of flight. Whether you're an experienced pilot or have never touched a drone before, you're welcome here. Join us to learn, build, fly, and explore the world from a new perspective.
